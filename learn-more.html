Your local “get‑it‑done” marketplace

handshake our purpose

TaskMart is a platform for small, local jobs and tasks. It connects people who need things done — chores, errands, small freelance gigs — with people who are willing to do them for a fee. Think of it as a hyper-local “get‑it‑done” marketplace, designed to build community and create easy earning opportunities.

how it works — key features

post_add

task posting

Users post tasks with details, location, and payment. Whether it’s delivering something, minor repairs, or tutoring — just describe what you need.

explore_nearby

browse & claim

Task doers browse nearby tasks and claim the ones that fit their skills and schedule. Quick, local, and flexible.

lock

safe payments

We hold payments in escrow until tasks are completed. Both parties feel secure — money is released only when the job is done right.

star

ratings & reviews

Task posters and doers rate each other, building trust and transparency across the community.

bolt

micro‑jobs focus

Unlike big freelancing sites, TaskMart is for quick, small, local jobs — delivering groceries, assembling furniture, walking dogs, and more.

payments

optional fees

We may charge a small fee per task to sustain the platform, while keeping it free for workers and posters to attract a vibrant community.

groups target users

📋 task posters

Busy people, small business owners, households — anyone needing quick help locally. Post a task, set a price, get it done.

🛠️ task doers

Students, gig workers, or anyone looking for short‑term, flexible side income. Browse tasks nearby and earn on your own time.

visibility our vision

TaskMart helps reduce crime and unemployment locally by creating easy, safe earning opportunities and fostering community connections. We believe small tasks can build big trust.
